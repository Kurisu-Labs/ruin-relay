## Environment spritesheet

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game environment spritesheet for a Phaser browser dungeon called Ruin Relay. Generate ONE square 1024x1024 PNG spritesheet, exactly a 4 by 4 grid of sixteen 256x256 cells, no borders, no labels, no text. Every cell is a separate reusable sprite; keep all objects entirely inside their own cell. Art direction: richly detailed hand-painted 2D dungeon game assets, weathered slate and sandstone, deep desaturated teal shadows, moss and roots, tiny warm amber accents, readable bold forms at small sizes. Camera: STRAIGHT orthographic top-down from the south, screen up is north, absolutely NO isometric diamonds or diagonal grid. A restrained visible SOUTH-facing wall lip adds depth, like a polished classic top-down dungeon crawler. Row 1: four different square floor surfaces that fill each whole cell edge to edge: worn irregular flagstones, cracked flagstones with sparse moss, dusty worn stone with subtle carved ruin symbols, aged cobbles with fine sand; keep all floors similarly dark, no chessboard alternating values, no outlines around entire tiles, no obstacles on floors. Row 2: four square wall blocks, tall carved ancient masonry seen from above with a short shadowed front lip, clearly solid obstacles, full 224x224 square footprint centered within transparent cell: old arch-carved stone block, mossy broken wall block, ornate ruined masonry with faded spiral relief, fractured stone block with embedded turquoise rune; similar dimensions so adjacent blocks form continuous walls. Row 3 transparent isolated decorative props: a broken round stone column with vines; a small tarnished brazier with amber fire; a cracked clay urn with fallen shards; a scattered small pile of skull and bones. Row 4 transparent isolated landmark/detail sprites: descending square stone stairwell seen from above; a circular carved stone portal floor with glowing turquoise runes, open walkable center; a ruined guardian statue bust on squat plinth; a small fern-and-rubble cluster. Render deliberate painterly textures and attractive detailed silhouettes, consistent light from upper left, professional cohesive game artwork. Floors must be opaque, all unused space in prop/wall cells genuinely transparent. No characters, no UI, no scenery/background, no ground plane outside sprites, no watermark.

## Adventurer spritesheet

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production animated character spritesheet for a straight top-down 2D dungeon crawler. Output square 1024x1024 PNG, exactly 4 columns by 4 rows, each cell 256x256, transparent background, no lines, labels or text. Same compact hooded adventurer in EVERY cell: weathered teal hood and short cloak, leather satchel, warm tiny amber lantern, dark boots, face partly hidden. Hand-painted polished indie game art, worn medieval ruin mood, clear chunky silhouette, consistent scale. Camera is orthographic straight from above with slight SOUTH-facing view to show the face of the south-facing character, classic overhead dungeon crawler, NO isometric or diagonal facing. All characters centered at identical center, feet baseline at y=208 in every cell; entire character height about 160px, width about 115px. Four cardinal directions ONLY. Row 1 faces NORTH, back of hood visible, moving straight screen up. Row 2 faces EAST, profile looking screen right. Row 3 faces SOUTH, face looking screen down. Row 4 faces WEST, profile looking screen left. Each row: column 1 neutral standing pose; column 2 left foot forward, right foot back; column 3 neutral passing stride; column 4 right foot forward, left foot back. Keep head and torso consistent, alternate feet and small cloak swing. No weapons, no big halos, no scenery, no floor, no cast background shadow, no grid marks, no UI, no logo, no watermark. True alpha transparency around every sprite. This sheet must be usable as sixteen fixed 256x256 atlas frames.
